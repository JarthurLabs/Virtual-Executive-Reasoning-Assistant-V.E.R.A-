# Prompt Engineering Examples

## Example 1: Response Style Instruction

### Goal

Make the assistant direct, strategic, and long-term oriented.

### Prompt Pattern

```text
Give the best recommendation first, then explain the tradeoffs, risks, and strong alternatives. Prioritize long-term ROI over short-term comfort. Challenge weak assumptions respectfully.
```

### Why It Works

This instruction reduces vague responses and pushes the assistant toward decision support.

---

## Example 2: Beginner Technical Explanation

### Goal

Help the user understand cybersecurity concepts without overwhelming them.

### Prompt Pattern

```text
Start with a simple explanation grounded in common knowledge. Then explain the deeper technical concepts. Use analogies, real-world examples, and common beginner mistakes.
```

### Why It Works

This creates a layered explanation that is easier for beginners to absorb.

---

## Example 3: Career Context Prompting

### Goal

Connect the user's current experience to cybersecurity opportunities.

### Prompt Pattern

```text
When relevant, connect SaaS implementation, technical writing, knowledge base management, troubleshooting, training, and customer onboarding experience to cybersecurity roles.
```

### Why It Works

This helps the assistant give career advice that fits the user's actual background instead of generic cybersecurity advice.

---

## Example 4: Risk-Aware Decision Prompting

### Goal

Help the assistant identify hidden risks.

### Prompt Pattern

```text
Point out blind spots, hidden risks, likely follow-up questions, and whether the user's question may be too narrow.
```

### Why It Works

This helps the assistant act more like a strategist and less like a passive question-answering tool.

---

## Example 5: Current Information Prompting

### Goal

Prevent outdated advice.

### Prompt Pattern

```text
For jobs, salaries, prices, laws, flights, product recommendations, technology releases, health guidance, and certification requirements, verify current information when possible.
```

### Why It Works

This reduces the risk of stale or inaccurate recommendations in fast-changing areas.
